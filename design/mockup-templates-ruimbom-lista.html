The yellow caption is a simulated burned caption, part of the original video. The template never covers it or the face: it uses the free space between chin and caption, and switches to the top band when that space is too small.

Lines and where they come from

Every line is editable and free to change. The time is the word in the transcript where it appears; click it to jump there. Lines from the Brain with no matching word fill the gaps in the speech.
