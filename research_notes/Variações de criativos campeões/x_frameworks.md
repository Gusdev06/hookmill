# How X/Twitter media buyers and creative strategists describe iterating winning ads (Meta and TikTok, 2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-12. Method note: direct fetches of x.com posts return HTTP 402 (blocked), so tweet text was only available through search-result snippets. Most of the substance comes from the long-form material that X voices link to: Motion talks and webinars (motionapp.com/library), newsletters (Alex Cooper/beehiiv) and LinkedIn. Motion pages are AI-generated session summaries. Treat their quotes as close paraphrase, not verbatim. Older material is labeled.

## 1. Which iteration frameworks are popular?

### Takeaway
The prevailing 2025-2026 framing: "iterate vs. diversify" are complementary, but after Andromeda an iteration only counts if it is *visually* different. Popular named structures are the "ad family" or "Creative Scaling Engine" (one winner deconstructed and multiplied), concept × hook matrices (about 10 concepts × 5-10 hooks), and modular hook/body mashups. Recommended net-new vs. iteration ratios conflict widely, from 90/10 to 50/50.

### Cited Findings
**Named frameworks**
- **Creative Scaling Engine (Viti Videtta, Happy Mammoth, Motion panel Jun 2026):** Winner → Deconstruct → Multiply → Test → Scale → Fatigue → Refresh. Deconstruct the winner into hook, angle, format and emotional driver. Multiply by changing hooks, creators, scripts, b-roll or format, to roughly 10-15+ variations per winner. — [Motion: Scaling UGC ads post-Andromeda](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Ad families / "Winner → iterate low-hanging fruit → expand" (Sophia Beauvoir, Ready Set, same panel):** build a family of ads from one winning ad. Post-Andromeda, the iteration step requires bigger changes and expansion targets new personas and pain points. Her sweet spot is 3-5 new batches per winner. She supports >$2M/month in spend. — [Motion panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Mashups (Sophia Beauvoir):** combine winning elements from different top performers, e.g. a high-thumbstop hook with a high-CVR body. This is the modular hook × body idea. — [Motion panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Concept × hook matrix (Caleb Kruse, Motion tutorial, Jan 2026):** "Launch 10 concepts with 5 to 10 hook variations each," which gives 50-100 ads/month. He replaces the old "10-15 ads/month to find 1-2 winners." — [Motion: 10 ads vs 100 ads](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-new-meta-ads-testing-strategy-10-ads-vs-100-ads/)
- **"Super iteration" (Dara Denney, Motion lightning round, Feb 2025):** treat a winner as a starting point. Her team took messaging from high-performing UGC and put it on a static, then tested 5 messaging variations on one image (grid layout). The winner, "The 5 Minute Makeup Routine," reached $2M+ spend and $6M+ revenue. — [Motion 2025 lightning round](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)
- **Dara Denney on X (Dec 2024):** "2023: creative strategy is the most important thing. 2024: creative diversity is the most important thing. 2025: creative VOLUME is the most important thing." — [X post](https://x.com/DenneyDara/status/1864706229731197235). In an Oct 2025 keynote she reportedly called creative diversity/variety one of the biggest performance factors. — [Motion keynote](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/why-most-creative-strategists-are-set-up-to-fail-and-how-to-fix-it/)
- **Identity matching (Sarah Levinger, Feb 2025):** combine 2-3 audience identities in one ad, researched from customer reviews, and keep a reusable archetype library. This is a persona-axis framework, not an iteration recipe. — [Motion 2025 lightning round](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)
- **Brand / Customer / Expert POV diversification (Mirella Crespi, Creative Milkshake, Feb 2025):** vary message, format, execution and placement across these POVs. — [Motion 2025 lightning round](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)
- **3:2:2 method (Charley Tichenor, original ~Dec 2023, OLDER):** 3 creatives × 2 primary texts × 2 headlines in one Dynamic Creative ad set, giving 12 combos. — [podcast listing, Dec 2023](https://podwise.ai/episodes/163760); structure described at [adadvisor](https://adadvisor.ai/docs/skills/adadvisor-creative). The same adadvisor page attributes a Sep 2025 statement to Tichenor that he "was wrong" about 3:2:2 after Andromeda and that top advertisers now test more concepts per segment. **Unverified against his own posts.**

**Net-new vs. iteration ratios (conflicting)**
- **90/10:** 90% net-new concepts informed by past performance, 10% small-tweak iterations on winners. Also argues 3-5 ads/month = slow learning and that top brands produce 50+ creatives/month. — [CreativeOS on X, Apr 2025](https://x.com/creativeosHQ/status/1913262756071194857) (snippet only; the post itself could not be fetched)
- **80/20 new concepts vs. hook iterations, plus ~2 new hooks/month split-tested on winners:** from a Savannah Sanchez Motion session summary. The page may date from 2024 (likely OLDER). — [Motion event: Secrets of high-converting ads feat. Savannah Sanchez](https://motionapp.com/event/secrets-of-high-converting-ads-feat-savannah-sanchez)
- **50/50:** Meta reportedly suggests 20 new creatives/week, half variations of existing visuals and half new concepts. This is secondhand and the Meta page is unverified. — [Dataiads](https://www.dataiads.io/en/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-velocity)
- **Motion's framing:** "diversification creates new territory (persona, angle, emotional world); iteration refines existing winners (hooks, copy, thumbnails). Both needed; only diversification generates new AI signal." — [Motion search summary of Andromeda sessions](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/andromeda-2-0-explained-how-meta-s-algorithm-actually-picks-your-winning-ads/)

### Inferences
- The hook × body × CTA modular idea survives mostly as "mashups" and hook stacks on a proven body. The 2025-26 consensus adds a constraint: each variant must change the visual, not just text.
- No authoritative net-new/iteration ratio exists. The spread from 90/10 to 50/50 reflects different account sizes and philosophies. A practical reading is that more net-new is needed when scale or new audiences are the goal and more iteration when a winner is still fresh.

### Gaps
- Could not read original X threads (402 on x.com, and threadreaderapp copies did not surface). No "50-5-3" framework was found under that name in any source.
- Tichenor's Sep 2025 reversal is not confirmed from a primary source.

## 2. Which levers to iterate on a winner first, and in what order?

### Takeaway
The common order is cheap and diagnostic levers first: hook/first 3 seconds (if thumbstop is low), footage refresh, length, value-prop order, headline/messaging on the same visual. Then talent/persona/creator swaps. Then format changes and a full "v2" (new script, hooks, visuals) when the winner fatigues. Use the metric diagnosis to decide which part to change.

### Cited Findings
- **Sophia Beauvoir order:** (1) low-hanging fruit: footage refresh from the existing library, length tests, value-prop reordering; (2) talent/persona refresh with a similar script; (3) complete talent and visual refresh to reach new personas; (4) mashups. — [Motion panel Jun 2026](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Viti Videtta (Happy Mammoth):** test formats in order: statics first, then short video, then testimonial/high production. When a winner fatigues, build a "v2" with new script, hooks, visuals and possibly a new format. Sheet color tags: green = winner (take bigger swings), yellow = already scaled (smaller swings). — [Motion panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Janae LeVander (Caraway):** winning scripts get iterations varied by SKU, persona, hook and edit style. Use 3-5 hooks with new personas plus caption/editing-style changes. Test each new brief with 3 unique creator personas. Caraway's "negative hook" concept scaled from 1 concept to 10+ variations and became a top UGC ad in 2026. — [Motion panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Metric-led rule:** low thumbstop + high conversion means iterate only the first 3 seconds (attributed to host Evan Lee in a Savannah Sanchez session; likely OLDER). — [Motion event](https://motionapp.com/event/secrets-of-high-converting-ads-feat-savannah-sanchez)
- **Dara Denney:** same visual, different messages (headline/angle), then scale the winner. She maps messaging to the 5 awareness stages: the most scalable angles are top-of-funnel and offers convert best at the bottom. — [Motion 2025 lightning round](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)
- **Nik Sharma (Sharma Brands/CreativeOS):** rapid iteration doubles down on winning hooks, graphics and copy. Audience order is broad → lookalikes → interests. — [Motion 2025 lightning round](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)
- **Josh Bampton (Motion CSM, Jan 2026):** keep the proven persona and messaging combo but make each variation visually different (Before/After, Us vs. Them, Problem Agitation). Minor single-element changes (e.g., the CTA) are reserved for "near-working" ads. For new concepts, pick underused angles (<20 creatives) paired with underused visual formats. — [Motion: Andromeda demands this new ad structure](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/andromeda-demands-this-new-ad-structure-do-it-easily-with-motion/)
- **Caleb Kruse:** vary emotional angle (problem, social proof, contrarian, storytelling). Running the same angle as video, static and carousel does NOT count as concept diversity. — [Motion: 10 vs 100 ads](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-new-meta-ads-testing-strategy-10-ads-vs-100-ads/)
- **Savannah Sanchez (TikTok-native, Feb 2025):** hook-led iteration using visual hook devices (cake writing, handwriting, stop motion, ASMR, search bar, slot machine, multiple choice, triplets) and authenticity formats (match my outfit, car chat, what I bought). She offers 24 hook templates. Her team worked with 200+ brands in 2024 with 40 creators and 10 editors. — [Motion 2025 lightning round](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/). In 2026 she keeps a database of every tested hook with ad links. — [Motion: 15 UGC hooks (Jul 2026)](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/15-proven-ugc-ad-hooks-that-are-working-right-now-savannah-sanchez/)
- **Barry Hott (via Alex Cooper, Apr 2025):** judge hooks by relevance, not just hook rate, since some winning ads had hook rates under 15%. "Make the longest ad that you can that doesn't require any edits." Mimic organic content style (angles, lighting, audio, pacing), not competitors. Use one full-funnel ad for cold and warm. — [Alex Cooper newsletter](https://alexcooper.beehiiv.com/p/7-facebook-advertising-lessons-i-learned-from-barry-hott). Motion also built an AI agent "Barry" that finds hooks and statics already hiding in your account to relaunch. — [Motion expert agent](https://motionapp.com/expert-agents/barry)

### Inferences
- The consensus order is: hook/opening visual → body footage/length/value-prop order → creator/persona → format → full v2. Use thumbstop vs. CVR to decide whether the front or the back of the ad is broken.
- Post-Andromeda, "hook iteration" increasingly means a new opening *visual* or a new creator, not new text overlays.

### Gaps
- No source gave a quantified ranking such as "hook swaps win X% of the time vs. body swaps."
- Very little TikTok-specific iteration order beyond Savannah Sanchez's hook catalog.

## 3. How do they structure creative testing (ABO/CBO, ads per ad set, budgets, kill/scale thresholds)?

### Takeaway
2026 voices lean toward consolidated CBO with ad sets grouped by concept or persona and many ads per ad set (10-20), letting Meta allocate. A minority keeps separate testing campaigns with fixed windows and moves winners to scaling. Concrete budget and kill numbers are rarely published.

### Cited Findings
- **Caleb Kruse (Jan 2026):** campaign-level CBO; ad sets grouped by concept or persona, not format; 10-20 ad variations per ad set. Optionally set a minimum ad-spend threshold to force delivery to new creative (no value given). 4-week cadence: wk1 3-5 new concepts × 3-5 hooks (15-20 ads); wk2 iterate top performers (10-15); wk3 2-3 new concepts + iterations (10-15); wk4 review. No kill rules given. — [Motion: 10 vs 100 ads](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-new-meta-ads-testing-strategy-10-ads-vs-100-ads/)
- **Josh Bampton (Jan 2026):** ad sets organized by persona/concept rather than funnel stage, with multiple variations per ad set and Andromeda choosing. About 50 optimization events to exit learning. Example product demo ad: 23 days, $3,684 spend, 22.04% thumbstop, 8.55% hold, 1.68% CTR. — [Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/andromeda-demands-this-new-ad-structure-do-it-easily-with-motion/)
- **Janae LeVander (Caraway):** simpler, broader structures with consolidated budgets; avoid many narrow ad sets. Runs ~115 live UGC ads/month (200+ in BFCM) and launches ~40 new/month (60+ in BFCM). Caraway UGC averages 2.14 ROAS (2026) with 358 live UGC ads. — [Motion panel Jun 2026](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Viti Videtta's definition of a winner:** stable CPA, strong CTR or hook rate, works across audiences, engagement, healthy ROAS, and spend picking up. — [Motion panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Sophia Beauvoir:** if an ad doesn't spend, the algorithm found no responsive pocket, so change the persona. During learning, watch hook rate, hold rate and spend. — [Motion panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- **Nik Sharma (Feb 2025):** a dedicated test program; 5-7 day test windows, then kill losers; graduate creatives to scaling campaigns at a 3x CPA target threshold (as summarized). Only 10-25% of creatives find scale. "Creative is the new targeting." — [Motion 2025 lightning round](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)
- **Ash Melwani (Obvi; LinkedIn, ~Feb 2024, OLDER):** group each test by angle with 3-6 visually distinct ads per angle; launch midweek; move winners into scaling campaigns the following week. — [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ashvinmelwani_i-often-get-asked-how-i-approach-testing-activity-7166786175318347777-mRD3)
- **Tichenor 3:2:2 (OLDER, 2023):** one Dynamic Creative ad set with 12 combos per test. Reportedly superseded post-Andromeda (unverified). — [adadvisor](https://adadvisor.ai/docs/skills/adadvisor-creative)

**Volumes cited**
- Happy Mammoth: ~500 ads/week; each strategist 20-30 unique ads/week; concepts cut from 120-130 to 80-90/week post-Andromeda (quality over quantity). — [Motion panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- Caleb Kruse: 50-100 ads/month; predicts 4x+ more creative tested in 2026 vs 2024 (a prediction). — [Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-new-meta-ads-testing-strategy-10-ads-vs-100-ads/)
- CreativeOS: 50+ creatives/month at top brands. — [X](https://x.com/creativeosHQ/status/1913262756071194857)
- Common Thread Co.: brands with 20+ new ads/month see 65% higher ROAS than those with <10 (cites a 1M+ creative study that is not linked; treat cautiously). — [Common Thread](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/meta-andromeda-roas-creative-strategy-2026)
- Motion 2026 Creative Benchmarks: 550k+ ads, 6,000+ advertisers, ~$1.3B spend. Top hook tactics for $1M+/month spenders are confession, bold claim, relatability, contrast and curiosity. — [Motion benchmarks](https://motionapp.com/events/2026-creative-strategy-bootcamp/homebase/2026-creative-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- The structural shift from 2023-24 to 2026: from isolated ABO test campaigns plus Dynamic Creative toward consolidated CBO "concept/persona" ad sets stuffed with visually distinct variants. Rigorous kill thresholds are mostly left to each account's CPA.
- Happy Mammoth cutting concepts while keeping ~500 ads/week suggests the elite pattern is fewer concepts with many visually distinct executions per concept.

### Gaps
- No X voice found publishing specific per-test budgets (e.g., "$X/day per ad set") or numeric kill rules for 2025-26, apart from Nik Sharma's 5-7 days / 3x CPA.
- The "3x CPA" threshold wording in the Motion summary is ambiguous (spend of 3x CPA vs. hitting CPA target).

## 4. What do they say no longer works in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
Near-duplicates and text-only tweaks are clustered by Andromeda and treated as one ad. Hook-only text changes don't create a new winner. Narrow ad-set splits, polished production and 3:2:2-style Dynamic Creative micro-tests are described as outdated.

### Cited Findings
- "Running 10 versions of the same image with minor text tweaks": Andromeda clusters them as one. Also "changing only a hook or opening visual and expecting a new winner." — Sophia Beauvoir, [Motion panel Jun 2026](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- Making only slight changes, and scaling the ad instead of the *mechanism* behind it. — Viti Videtta, [same panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- Many narrow ad sets; forcing every creator into the same mold. — Janae LeVander, [same panel](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/scaling-ugc-ads-post-andromeda-one-winner-many-formats/)
- Text-only changes on the same visual don't count as new hooks. Generic creatives with no clear persona struggle. Mass-producing concepts without strategy is a mistake, and so is abandoning iteration entirely. — Josh Bampton, [Motion Jan 2026](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/andromeda-demands-this-new-ad-structure-do-it-easily-with-motion/)
- Launching 10-15 ads/month to find 1-2 winners is obsolete. Reformatting one angle (video/static/carousel) is not diversity. — Caleb Kruse, [Motion Jan 2026](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-new-meta-ads-testing-strategy-10-ads-vs-100-ads/)
- Traditional micro-variation testing ("small tweaks, endless iterations") "can't keep up". Similar-looking ads are grouped and penalized. — [Segwise (vendor)](https://segwise.ai/blog/beat-meta-andromeda-algorithm-creative-strategy)
- 3:2:2 Dynamic Creative: Tichenor reportedly recanted in Sep 2025 (unverified). — [adadvisor](https://adadvisor.ai/docs/skills/adadvisor-creative)
- Polished, high-production ads are out and authentic/"ugly" is in. — Barry Hott and Savannah Sanchez, [Motion 2025 lightning round](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/). Classic hold/unbox influencer UGC is "slowly dying." — Mirella Crespi, [same](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)
- Letting 1-2 creatives carry an account's spend; letting tests run past 5-7 days. — Nik Sharma, [same](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)

### Inferences
- For a variation generator, every "variation" of a winner should change at least one *visual* dimension (creator/face, setting, opening shot, format). Text-only hook swaps should be treated as low-value under Andromeda.

### Gaps
- Few sources speak about Dynamic Creative / Advantage+ creative directly in 2025-26. Josh Bampton's talk does not mention it.
- TikTok-specific "what stopped working" claims were not found from X voices.

## 5. Reference voices and what they said (roster)

### Takeaway
The X-linked reference voices concentrate around Motion's talk library (Dara Denney, Savannah Sanchez, Barry Hott, Sarah Levinger, Nik Sharma, Mirella Crespi, Caleb Kruse) plus operators showcased on its panels (Happy Mammoth, Caraway, Ready Set). Foreplay and CreativeOS act as amplifiers on X.

### Cited Findings
- **Dara Denney (Motion):** volume is the priority in 2025 ([X](https://x.com/DenneyDara/status/1864706229731197235)); "super iterations" with 5 messaging variations per visual, giving $2M+ spend/$6M+ revenue ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)).
- **Barry Hott:** "Just Make Ads," ugly/native, relevance over hook rate; $600M+ spend per Motion ([Motion agent](https://motionapp.com/expert-agents/barry)), $1B per Alex Cooper ([newsletter](https://alexcooper.beehiiv.com/p/7-facebook-advertising-lessons-i-learned-from-barry-hott)). The two spend figures conflict across sources.
- **Sarah Levinger:** identity matching, 2-3 identities per ad ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)).
- **Savannah Sanchez:** TikTok-native hook devices and hook database ([Motion 2025](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/), [Motion 2026](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/15-proven-ugc-ad-hooks-that-are-working-right-now-savannah-sanchez/)).
- **Nik Sharma:** test → scale pipeline, 10-25% of creatives scale ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/motion-s-2025-facebook-ad-creative-trends-dtc-expert-lightning-round/)).
- **Charley Tichenor:** 3:2:2 (2023) and its reported reversal (2025) ([adadvisor](https://adadvisor.ai/docs/skills/adadvisor-creative)).
- **Caleb Kruse:** 10 concepts × 5-10 hooks, CBO with 10-20 ads/ad set ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-new-meta-ads-testing-strategy-10-ads-vs-100-ads/)).
- **Ash Melwani (Obvi):** 3-6 visually distinct ads per angle, weekly cadence (2024) ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ashvinmelwani_i-often-get-asked-how-i-approach-testing-activity-7166786175318347777-mRD3)).
- **Nick Shackelford (Structured Social / Foundr Operators):** builds repeatable creative testing systems. No specific 2025-26 numbers found. — [Foundr](https://foundr.com/pages/ad-testing), [MentorPass](https://www.mentorpass.co/shack)
- **CreativeOS (X account):** 90/10 net-new vs. iterations; 50+ creatives/month ([X](https://x.com/creativeosHQ/status/1913262756071194857)).
- **Foreplay:** hosts expert pages and events (Barry Hott, Savannah Sanchez) but no distinct iteration framework was found. — [Foreplay Barry Hott](https://www.foreplay.co/experts/barry-hott), [Foreplay Savannah event](https://www.foreplay.co/events/social-savanah)

### Inferences
- Motion's library is the de facto long-form layer behind much X discourse on this topic. Its June-July 2026 sessions are the most current, post-Andromeda statements.
